# Problem Statement

Access to healthcare is influenced by the spatial distribution of health facilities, 
population, transportation networks, and terrain. This analysis assesses geographic 
accessibility to health facilities in Nairobi County by examining population 
distribution, road proximity, facility locations, administrative areas, and elevation.

## Objectives
1. Where are the health facilities?
2. Where are the populations?
3. Which populated areas are relatively close to health facilities and roads?
4. Which populated areas appear underserved, particularly where terrain may make 
   physical access more difficult?
